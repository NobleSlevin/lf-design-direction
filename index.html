Prestige Editorial

Cinematic full-bleed photography against deep charcoal or off-white. Serif headlines carry weight and gravitas. Gold/brass accents as the sole warm note in an otherwise cool palette. White-space is generous — the brand says little, but says it loudly.

Modern Authority

Deep forest green or midnight navy as the hero canvas. Warm cream/parchment as secondary surface. Rounded pill CTAs introduce softness that prevents the palette from feeling cold. Mixes editorial serif with clean sans — approachable authority.

Noir Persona

Near-black canvas that's nearly a void. A single portrait photograph takes the entire stage — person, not iconography. Ultra-bold grotesque type bleeds over the image. One accent color dot. This is personal brand energy: one name, one face, total presence.
